How it works

25 players max

Like a real amateur club. On match day Leo takes 20: eleven starters and nine on the bench.

Every player costs €50

Registration with the league plus the sports medical. The bank decides how many we can sign.

Every €50 signs the next one

The bank grows with likes and views on every episode. When it hits €50, the next number on the list signs. Leo can call by position if he needs a goalkeeper.

Get a number

Comment I'M IN + your position under our latest episode. Your number is the order you applied in. Following us is never required.
